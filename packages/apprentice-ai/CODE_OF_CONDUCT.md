# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing another person’s data or credentials are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers will minimize disclosure and avoid conflicts of interest when handling reports.

This policy applies to project spaces and public representation of the project.
