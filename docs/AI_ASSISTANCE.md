# AI assistance disclosure

The initial 0.1.0 implementation and documentation were produced with substantial AI coding assistance under human direction. Generated suggestions were not treated as evidence: behaviors were exercised through fresh unit, subprocess, HTTP, raw-database, archive-adversarial, reproducibility, and distribution tests.

Contributors should disclose material AI assistance in pull requests, identify generated areas, and remain responsible for correctness, licensing, privacy, and security. Never provide an AI tool with real credentials or user observations for this project.
